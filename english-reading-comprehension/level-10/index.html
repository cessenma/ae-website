國小英文閱讀測驗第 10 級
A Year on the Wall

讀整段故事的孩子適合這一級。先讀故事、聽朗讀，再回答 5 題；答完立刻看到分數和每一題的中文解說，不用註冊。

先讀故事

先讀 A Year on the Wall

這是 Chris and Cora 分級讀本第 10 級第一本的前 4 頁（313 個字）。每一頁都可以按「聽這一頁」，聽美式發音朗讀。

"A new year," said Mom on the first morning of January. "Twelve months, each a page. We make the year on paper and put it on the wall." "Why?" asked Cora. "So we do not forget a thing. Birthdays, everything." "Can I draw on it?" "You draw, Chris writes the words, and I say the days." "Right, then everything is on the wall, and we do not lose a thing," said Chris.

First, the kitchen clock, on the table. "A day goes round the clock two times," she said. "From twelve at night to noon is a.m., and from noon to twelve at night is p.m." "So eleven in the morning is eleven a.m., and eleven at night is eleven p.m.," said Chris. "And noon?" asked Cora, and Mom said noon is twelve, when the morning stops and the afternoon begins.

"Now the small numbers," said Mom. "Half past eleven is eleven and a half, and a quarter past is eleven and a quarter." "What is a quarter?" asked Cora. "One of four, like a quarter of a cake." "And a half is one of two," said Chris. "That is many numbers, give me a moment," said Cora. She looked at the clock for a moment, and a moment more. "I am sure now, the half is big, the quarter is small, and it begins at a.m." "Right, easy," said Mom.

Then the days. Chris wrote them on the first page in a row, Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday, Friday, Saturday, Sunday. "Seven days, and the week begins on Monday," said Mom. "Monday to Friday is school, Saturday and Sunday are for us." "Which day is the best?" asked Cora. "Friday," said Chris, "because Saturday comes after it." "Sunday," said Cora, "because Monday comes after it." "That is not my answer," said Chris. "It is my answer, and the seven days are mine too."

回答問題

讀完了？回答 5 題

答案都在上面的故事裡。可以回去再看一次，答完按「對答案」，每題都有中文解說。

Where will they put the year on paper?

媽媽說「put it on the wall」，要把年曆貼在牆上。

Who writes the words?

媽媽說「Chris writes the words」，寫字的是 Chris。

What is eleven at night?

Chris 說「eleven at night is eleven p.m.」，晚上十一點是 p.m.。

What does "a quarter" mean here?

媽媽解釋 quarter 是「One of four」，四分之一，就像四分之一個蛋糕。

Why does Chris think Friday is the best day?

Chris 說星期五最好，「because Saturday comes after it」，因為隔天就是星期六。
