國小英文閱讀測驗第 8 級
The Birthday Surprise

讀得出短句的孩子適合這一級。先讀故事、聽朗讀，再回答 5 題；答完立刻看到分數和每一題的中文解說，不用註冊。

先讀故事

先讀 The Birthday Surprise

這是 Chris and Cora 分級讀本第 8 級第一本的前 4 頁（243 個字）。每一頁都可以按「聽這一頁」，聽美式發音朗讀。

"Next week is Cora's birthday," said Mom. "It is on the first day of the month. I think we can have a surprise party." "What is a surprise party?" asked Chris. "A party Cora does not know about. She does not see it all week, and then, surprise!" "Can I help?" "Yes, help me, but you must never tell Cora."

"Why a surprise?" asked Chris. "Because a surprise is fun," said Mom. "Every year we have a cake, and this year, this month, a party too." "What time?" "Three o'clock, so we must be ready before three o'clock." "I could draw a picture for her," said Chris. "Yes, draw her a birthday picture. And think of a game."

Chris drew a big round cake with seven candles. Then Cora came in. "What are you drawing?" she asked. Chris put the picture behind him. "Just a cake," he said. "Why just a cake?" "I just like round cakes." Cora looked at him and said, "You are hiding something!" and Chris went behind the sofa.

Mom and Chris went out to find a present for Cora. They found a dress, a red dress with white buttons in a row. "She will like this dress," said Mom. "Why?" "Because she likes red." Then they found a pair of socks, red socks to go with the dress. "A dress and a pair of socks, two things in one present!" said Chris. "I think she will like them."

回答問題

讀完了？回答 5 題

答案都在上面的故事裡。可以回去再看一次，答完按「對答案」，每題都有中文解說。

When is Cora's birthday?

媽媽說「Next week is Cora's birthday」，Cora 的生日在下星期。

What is a surprise party?

媽媽解釋驚喜派對是「A party Cora does not know about.」，就是 Cora 不知道的派對。

What time is the party?

媽媽說「Three o'clock」，派對在三點。

What did Chris draw?

故事說「Chris drew a big round cake with seven candles.」，他畫了有七根蠟燭的圓蛋糕。

Why does Mom think Cora will like the dress?

媽媽說「Because she likes red.」，因為 Cora 喜歡紅色。
