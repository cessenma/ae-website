一句話回答

狼來了的英文是 The Boy Who Cried Wolf

故事裡最有名的幾句，英文是這樣說的：

Wolf! Wolf!狼來了！狼來了！
to cry wolf謊報危險、亂喊狼來了（這個故事變成的英文片語）
No one believes a liar, even when he tells the truth.說謊的人就算說真話，也沒有人相信。

1 / 6

A boy looks after the sheep on a hill. Every day is the same. "This is boring," he says. "I want to have some fun."

有一個男孩在山坡上看羊。每天都一樣。他說：「好無聊喔，我想找點好玩的事。」

2 / 6

The boy runs to the village. "Wolf! Wolf!" he cries. "A wolf is eating the sheep!" The people stop work and run up the hill to help him.

男孩跑到村子裡大喊：「狼來了！狼來了！有一隻狼在吃羊！」大家放下工作，跑上山坡要幫他。

3 / 6

But there is no wolf. The boy laughs and laughs. "There is no wolf!" he says. The people are angry. "Do not cry wolf when there is no wolf," they say.

可是根本沒有狼。男孩笑個不停，說：「沒有狼啦！」大家很生氣，說：「沒有狼的時候，不要亂喊狼來了。」

4 / 6

The next day, the boy does it again. "Wolf! Wolf!" he cries. The people run up the hill again. Again there is no wolf, and again the boy laughs. The people are very angry now.

第二天，男孩又做了一次。他大喊：「狼來了！狼來了！」大家又跑上山坡。還是沒有狼，男孩又笑了。這次大家非常生氣。

5 / 6

Then one day, a real wolf comes. The boy is afraid. "Wolf! Wolf! Please help!" he cries. But this time, no one comes. "He is not telling the truth again," the people say.

後來有一天，真的狼來了。男孩很害怕，大喊：「狼來了！狼來了！拜託救命！」可是這一次，沒有人來。大家說：「他又在說謊了。」

6 / 6

The wolf runs after the sheep, and the sheep run away. The boy sits on the hill and cries. Now he knows: if you tell lies, no one believes you, even when you tell the truth.

狼追著羊跑，羊都跑掉了。男孩坐在山坡上哭。現在他知道了：常常說謊的人，就算說真話，也沒有人相信。

If you tell lies, no one believes you, even when you tell the truth.常常說謊的人，就算說真話也沒有人相信。
