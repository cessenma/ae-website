The Story of Nian 英文故事，中英對照

1 / 6

Long ago, a monster called Nian lives in the sea. On the last night of every year, it comes to a small village. It eats the animals, and all the people are afraid.

很久很久以前，海裡住著一隻叫做「年」的怪獸。每年的最後一個晚上，牠都會來到一個小村子。牠會吃掉動物，大家都很害怕。

2 / 6

So every year, on that night, the people run away to the mountains. They take their children and their animals. No one stays in the village.

所以每年的那個晚上，大家都逃到山上去，帶著小孩和動物。村子裡一個人也不留。

3 / 6

One year, an old man comes to the village. "I will stay here tonight," he says. "I am not afraid of Nian." "No! Come with us!" say the people. But the old man smiles and stays.

有一年，一位老爺爺來到村子裡。他說：「今晚我要留在這裡，我不怕年獸。」大家說：「不行！跟我們走吧！」可是老爺爺笑一笑，留了下來。

4 / 6

At night, Nian comes. But what is this? The door is red. There is red paper on the house. Inside, the lights are bright. Nian does not like the color red. It stops.

到了晚上，年獸來了。咦，這是什麼？門是紅色的，房子上貼著紅紙，屋子裡燈火通明。年獸不喜歡紅色，牠停了下來。

5 / 6

Then the old man lights firecrackers. Bang! Bang! Bang! Nian is afraid of the loud noise. It runs back to the sea, and it does not come back.

接著老爺爺點燃了鞭炮。砰！砰！砰！年獸很怕這麼大的聲音。牠逃回海裡，再也沒有回來。

6 / 6

In the morning, the people come home. The village is safe! Now they know: Nian is afraid of red, bright lights, and loud noise. So every Lunar New Year, people put up red paper, light firecrackers, and say "Happy New Year!"

早上，大家回到家。村子平安無事！現在他們知道了：年獸怕紅色、怕亮光、怕大聲。所以每到農曆新年，大家都貼紅紙、放鞭炮，還互相說「新年快樂！」

This is why people use red paper and firecrackers at Lunar New Year.這就是過年貼紅紙、放鞭炮的由來。

Where does Nian live?

Where do the people go on that night?

故事說「the people run away to the mountains」，大家逃到山上。

Who stays in the village?

故事說「But the old man smiles and stays.」，老爺爺留了下來。

What color does Nian not like?

故事說「Nian does not like the color red.」，年獸不喜歡紅色。

Why does Nian run back to the sea?
