一句話回答

龜兔賽跑的英文是 The Tortoise and the Hare

故事裡最有名的幾句，英文是這樣說的：

Slow and steady wins the race.慢慢來、穩穩走，才會贏得比賽。
Ready, set, go!預備，開始！
Let us have a race.我們來比賽吧。
故事全文

The Tortoise and the Hare 英文故事，中英對照

1 / 6

A hare is very fast. He laughs at a tortoise. "You are so slow!" he says. "Slow?" says the tortoise. "Let us have a race." All the animals come to watch.

有一隻兔子跑得很快。他笑烏龜：「你好慢喔！」烏龜說：「慢嗎？那我們來比賽吧。」所有的動物都來看。

2 / 6

"Ready, set, go!" says the fox. The hare runs very fast. Soon he is far away. The tortoise walks slowly. Step by step, he does not stop.

狐狸說：「預備，開始！」兔子跑得非常快，一下子就跑得很遠了。烏龜慢慢地走，一步一步，都沒有停下來。

3 / 6

The hare looks back. He cannot see the tortoise. "He is so slow," says the hare. "I have time for a little sleep." He sits under a big tree and closes his eyes.

兔子回頭看，看不到烏龜。兔子說：「他好慢喔，我還有時間睡一下。」他坐在一棵大樹下，閉上了眼睛。

4 / 6

The tortoise walks and walks. He is hot and tired, but he does not stop. He walks by the big tree. The hare is sleeping. The tortoise keeps on walking.

烏龜走啊走。他又熱又累，可是沒有停下來。他走過那棵大樹，兔子正在睡覺。烏龜繼續往前走。

5 / 6

The hare wakes up. "Oh no!" he says. He runs as fast as he can. But it is too late. The tortoise is at the finish line.

兔子醒來了。他說：「糟糕！」他用最快的速度跑。可是太晚了，烏龜已經到終點線了。

6 / 6

"I win!" says the tortoise. All the animals clap. The hare is sad. "I was fast, but I stopped," he says. "You were slow, but you did not stop."

烏龜說：「我贏了！」所有的動物都拍手。兔子很難過。他說：「我跑得快，可是我停下來了。你走得慢，可是你沒有停。」

Slow and steady wins the race.穩穩地走、不停下來的人，才會贏得比賽。

讀懂了嗎？5 題

Why does the hare laugh at the tortoise?

兔子說「You are so slow!」，他笑烏龜走得慢。

Who says "Ready, set, go"?

故事說「"Ready, set, go!" says the fox.」，喊開始的是狐狸。

What does the hare do under the big tree?

兔子坐在大樹下、閉上眼睛，後面也說「The hare is sleeping.」，他在睡覺。

Why does the tortoise win?

烏龜又熱又累，可是「he does not stop」，一直走沒有停，所以贏了。

How does the hare feel at the end?

故事最後說「The hare is sad.」，兔子很難過。
