官方題型說明 Task format

八段互不相關的短錄音，各問一題。問的是大意、目的、功能、感受或態度——幾乎不問單純的事實。官方考卷中為 第 1–8 題，整份聽力卷約 40 分鐘・30 題。

題目說明（rubric）依照官方題型格式以英文呈現；以下練習題為埃森美語原創編寫，用於熟悉題型，非劍橋官方試題。官方免費樣卷：cambridgeenglish.org → B2 First for Schools preparation。

You will hear people talking in eight different situations. For questions 1–8, choose the best answer (A, B or C).

關於這些錄音　音檔為本地生成的神經網路合成語音，每個角色用不同的聲音、涵蓋多種英語口音，語速依等級調整，內容與停頓完全依照官方格式，包含官方規定的「播放兩次」。要誠實說一件事：合成語音再自然，也不等於真人錄音——正式考試放的是真人錄的。先自己聽兩次再打開逐字稿，效果差很多。

NarratorYou will hear eight short recordings. For each question, choose the best answer, A, B or C.

NarratorExtract one. You hear two friends talking about a football match.

WomanSo how did the match go on Saturday? I heard City won.

ManYeah, they did, by two points in the end. I mean, I'm glad we won, obviously, but if I'm honest, we were lucky more than anything. Their goalkeeper made about three mistakes in the second half.

WomanStill, a win's a win, right?

ManTrue, true. I just wish we'd actually played well for once instead of relying on the other side falling apart.

NarratorNow listen again.

WomanSo how did the match go on Saturday? I heard City won.

ManYeah, they did, by two points in the end. I mean, I'm glad we won, obviously, but if I'm honest, we were lucky more than anything. Their goalkeeper made about three mistakes in the second half.

WomanStill, a win's a win, right?

ManTrue, true. I just wish we'd actually played well for once instead of relying on the other side falling apart.

1. What is the boy's attitude to his team's win?

A：關鍵句是『we were lucky more than anything』與『I wish we'd actually played well for once』，兩句都指出勝利靠運氣而非實力，是正確答案。

B：男生說『we were lucky more than anything』（純粹是運氣好），並沒有表達以踢得好為傲，這是語氣誤判的干擾項——他態度保留，並非驕傲。

C：男生完全沒有提到比分差距的問題，這是『說了但不是問題所問』的干擾項——他不滿的是踢法內容，不是分數多寡。

2. Why is Amy calling the café?

A：Amy 說『I still really want to come in and talk to you about the position』，明確表示她仍想應徵，並非取消申請，是說反話方向的干擾項。

C：說話者從頭到尾都在使用這個 app（『I've started using this app』），並沒有停止使用，此選項與事實相反。

Woman 2When I signed up for the coastal clean-up last weekend, I honestly expected it to be depressing — three hours picking up other people's rubbish. For the first half hour, it kind of was; the amount of plastic on that one small beach was shocking. But then something shifted. Woman 2When I signed up for the coastal clean-up last weekend, I honestly expected it to be depressing — three hours picking up other people's rubbish. For the first half hour, it kind of was; the amount of plastic on that one small beach was shocking. But then something shifted. Everyone started calling out whenever they found something strange, turning it into a weird competition, and by the end we were laughing about who'd found the oddest shoe. I walked away feeling far more hopeful than when I arrived — like this problem isn't too big for ordinary people to do something about.

5. How does the speaker feel by the end of the clean-up?

A：說話者說海灘上的垃圾量『shocking』，代表撿到的垃圾很多而非太少，此選項與事實相反。

B：關鍵句是『I walked away feeling far more hopeful than when I arrived』，這是正確答案——她原本預期會覺得沮喪，結果反而更樂觀。

C：她確實提到大家在笑（amused 屬實），但她最後認為『this problem isn't too big for ordinary people to do something about』，代表她不覺得問題無法解決，與選項後半部矛盾，是『說對一半、扭曲另一半』的干擾項。

A：教練的說法其實偏向對己方有利（對手更不適應下雨），並沒有暗示球隊會輸，此選項與原文語氣相反。

C：關鍵句是『Don't let that get in your heads』與『a bit of rain tends to slow the other side down more than us』，整段話的目的是讓球隊對場地與天氣感到安心，是正確答案。
