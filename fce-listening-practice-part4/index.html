長訪談三選一

官方題型說明 Task format

一段長訪談，七題三選一。問題順序和訪談一致；考意見、態度與整體想法，需要撐住四五分鐘的專注。官方考卷中為 第 24–30 題，整份聽力卷約 40 分鐘・30 題。

題目說明（rubric）依照官方題型格式以英文呈現；以下練習題為埃森美語原創編寫，用於熟悉題型，非劍橋官方試題。官方免費樣卷：cambridgeenglish.org → B2 First for Schools preparation。

You will hear an interview. For questions 24–30, choose the best answer (A, B or C).

關於這些錄音　音檔為本地生成的神經網路合成語音，每個角色用不同的聲音、涵蓋多種英語口音，語速依等級調整，內容與停頓完全依照官方格式，包含官方規定的「播放兩次」。要誠實說一件事：合成語音再自然，也不等於真人錄音——正式考試放的是真人錄的。先自己聽兩次再打開逐字稿，效果差很多。

看逐字稿（先自己聽兩次再打開）

NarratorYou will hear an interview in which a radio host talks to a boy called Daniel about a school exchange programme he did in Taiwan. For questions one to seven, choose the best answer, A, B or C.

WomanNow, in the studio today I've got Daniel, who's just come back from five months at a school in Bristol, in Taiwan, as part of a student exchange programme. Daniel, welcome back! First off — of all the places you could have chosen, why Taiwan?

ManThanks for having me. Honestly, it wasn't my first choice — I'd originally put down Japan, but the places filled up incredibly fast, and Taiwan was the programme's second suggestion. I nearly said no, since I didn't know much about it, but a teacher convinced me to at least read about it before deciding — and once I did, I was more excited about Taiwan than I'd ever been about Japan.

WomanThat's a great turnaround. What surprised you most once you actually got there?

ManThe school days, definitely. Back home we finish around half three, but a lot of my classmates stayed at school, or went to what they call buxiban — extra evening classes — until nine or ten at night. I found that genuinely shocking. I kept asking my host brother when he got to relax, and he just laughed and said this was completely normal for him.

WomanDid you end up doing the same schedule?

ManNot the full thing — my host family said I didn't have to go to buxiban most nights. But I did go a couple of times a week, mostly out of curiosity, and it helped my Mandarin more than any of my actual lessons did.

WomanLet's talk about the language. How was that side of things?

ManRough at the start. For the first month I could barely order food without pointing at a menu. But my host mum was patient — she'd repeat things slowly without making me feel stupid about it, and that made a massive difference. By the end, I could get through a normal dinner conversation, enough to feel part of the family rather than just a guest.

WomanAnd what about the food? Any favourites, or anything you just couldn't get used to?

ManSo many favourites — beef noodle soup became a bit of an obsession. The one thing I never got fully used to was breakfast. Back home it's toast or cereal, but there it might be warm soy milk with a fried dough stick, or a bowl of rice porridge. It's not that I disliked it, it just took months before it stopped feeling strange to eat something savoury that early.

WomanAny particular moment from the trip that's stuck with you?

ManThere's one I keep coming back to. About three months in, a typhoon warning cancelled school for a whole day, which never happens back home. My host family and I ended up just cooking together the entire afternoon instead, and oddly, that ordinary, unplanned afternoon taught me more about how the family actually worked day to day than any of the trips we'd deliberately planned.

WomanThat's lovely. Final question — what would you say to another student who's thinking about doing an exchange but isn't sure?

ManDon't overthink the destination too much. I nearly turned Taiwan down because it wasn't the country I'd pictured, and it ended up being the best decision I didn't really make on purpose. The place matters less than just actually going, and letting yourself be a bit uncomfortable for the first few weeks.

1. Why did Daniel end up going to Taiwan?

A：關鍵句是『the places filled up incredibly fast』，代表他的第一志願（日本）名額已滿，才改成台灣，是正確答案。

B：Daniel 明確說『I'd originally put down Japan』，日本才是他原本的第一志願，此選項與原文相反。

C：老師只是『convinced me to at least read about it before deciding』（說服他先做功課再決定），並沒有直接『推薦台灣勝過日本』，此選項誇大了原文內容。

2. What did Daniel find most surprising when he arrived?

A：原文說台灣同學『stayed at school, or went…until nine or ten at night』，代表他們結束得比英國晚很多，並非提早，此選項與原文相反。

B：關鍵句是『a lot of my classmates stayed at school, or went straight to...buxiban...until nine or ten at night』，加上他說『I found that genuinely shocking』，這正是他感到驚訝的地方，是正確答案。

C：host brother 也是上課到晚上九、十點，還『laughed and said this was completely normal』，他的夜晚並不輕鬆，此選項與原文相反。

3. Why did Daniel go to buxiban himself sometimes?

A：關鍵句是『mostly out of curiosity』與『it helped my Mandarin more than any of my actual lessons did』，他去的原因是好奇，而且意外發現對中文有幫助，是正確答案。

B：原文沒有提到他去是為了『驗證補習班有多難』，此選項無依據。

C：host family 說『I didn't have to go to buxiban most nights』，並沒有要求他每晚都去，此選項與原文相反。

4. What does Daniel say about his host mother?

A：原文沒有提到 host mum『在家人面前糾正他的錯誤』，此選項無依據。

B：關鍵句是『she'd repeat things slowly without making me feel stupid about it』，正好對應『放慢速度且不讓他尷尬』，是正確答案。

C：原文完全沒有提到 host mum 是否說英文或拒絕說英文，此選項無中生有。

5. How does Daniel feel about Taiwanese breakfasts now?

A：他說『it just took months before it stopped feeling strange』，代表後來已經習慣了，說他始終覺得怪與原文相反。

B：他明確澄清『It's not that I disliked it』，豆漿油條、稀飯只是不習慣而非討厭，此選項與原文相反。

C：關鍵句是『it just took months before it stopped feeling strange』加上『It's not that I disliked it』，代表他花時間才習慣，但並不討厭，是正確答案。

6. What did Daniel value most about the typhoon day?

A：颱風假只是這件事的背景，他真正強調的是那個下午學到的事，並非放假本身，屬於『說了但不是問題所問』的干擾項。

B：他只說一起煮了一下午的飯，沒有提到學會任何菜色，重點在相處而非廚藝，此選項偏離重點。

C：關鍵句是『that ordinary, unplanned afternoon taught me more about how the family actually worked day to day than any of the trips we'd deliberately planned』，他重視的是這個意外、日常的相處時刻，是正確答案。

7. What is Daniel's main piece of advice for students considering an exchange?

A：關鍵句是『don't overthink the destination too much』與『The place matters less than just actually going』，這正是他給的核心建議，是正確答案。

B：他自己就是對台灣『didn't know much about it』才去的，反而是正面經驗，此選項與他的建議相反。

C：他自己就是去了不是原本選擇的台灣，而且認為這是『the best decision I didn't really make on purpose』，此選項與他的親身經驗和建議完全相反。
