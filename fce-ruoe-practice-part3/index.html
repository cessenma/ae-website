官方題型說明 Task format

給你字根，要你變出句子需要的詞性——名詞、形容詞、副詞，還要判斷需不需要加否定前綴。八題，全劍橋考試裡最能靠練習提分的一題。官方考卷中為 第 17–24 題。

題目說明（rubric）依照官方題型格式以英文呈現；以下練習題為埃森美語原創編寫，用於熟悉題型，非劍橋官方試題。官方免費樣卷：cambridgeenglish.org → B2 First for Schools preparation。

Read the text below. Use the word given in capitals at the end of some of the lines to form a word that fits in the gap in the same line.

Has Social Media Changed Friendship?

The _____(1)_____ of social media apps among teenagers has grown enormously over the past decade, and with it, the way young people make and keep friends has changed too.

It is hardly _____(2)_____ that many students now spend more time chatting online than meeting face to face. However, some experts argue that online _____(3)_____ are often less meaningful than the ones built in person, at school or on a sports team.

Teenagers who check their phones constantly may become _____(4)_____ to focus on their homework for more than a few minutes at a time. _____(5)_____, though, social media has also made the world feel smaller, letting students keep in touch with relatives and classmates who have moved overseas.

Many teens today value their online _____(6)_____ with friends around the world just as much as face-to-face contact. Interestingly, many only reach this _____(7)_____ after taking a short break from their phones. Constant notifications, researchers add, can also make people _____(8)_____, unable to give their full attention to a single task for very long.

1. 第 (1) 格　POPULAR

解析：形容詞 popular 加上字尾 -ity 轉為抽象名詞 popularity（受歡迎程度）；空格在 The ___ of... 之後需要一個名詞當主詞。

2. 第 (2) 格　SURPRISE

解析：句型 It is hardly ___ that... 的補語需要形容詞，名詞/動詞 surprise 加上 -ing 轉為形容詞 surprising（令人驚訝的）；不可用過去分詞 surprised，因為主詞 it 不會『感到驚訝』，只會『使人驚訝』。

3. 第 (3) 格　FRIEND

解析：名詞 friend 加上字尾 -ship 轉為 friendship（友誼）；主詞 online ___ are 搭配複數動詞 are，故需再加 -s 變成複數 friendships。

4. 第 (4) 格　ABLE

解析：形容詞 able 加上否定字首 un- 轉為 unable（無法的），符合『無法專心』的語意；這是 word formation 常考的『隱藏否定』陷阱之一。

5. 第 (5) 格　ARGUE

解析：動詞 argue 先轉為形容詞 arguable，再加上字尾 -ly 轉為副詞 arguably（可以說、可論證地），放在句首修飾整句話。

6. 第 (6) 格　CONNECT

解析：動詞 connect 加上字尾 -ion 轉為名詞 connection（連結）；因主詞 online ___ with friends 語意上指多個對象的連結，複數 connections 更自然，但不可數用法 connection 亦視為正確。

7. 第 (7) 格　REALISE

解析：動詞 realise 加上字尾 -ation 轉為名詞 realisation（體悟、領悟）；劍橋考試採英式拼法 realisation，但美式拼法 realization 在此也視為正確。

8. 第 (8) 格　PATIENT

解析：形容詞 patient 加上否定字首 im-（用於以 p 開頭的字，而非 un-）轉為 impatient（不耐煩的）；否定字首 im-/il-/ir-/in-/un- 會依字首字母變化，是 word formation 常考重點。
