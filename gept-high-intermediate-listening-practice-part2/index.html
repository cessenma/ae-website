全民英檢中高級聽力題目
第二部分　對話

依全民英檢中高級聽力測驗格式出題，共 15 題。做完按「對答案」，每一題都有中文解析：為什麼對、為什麼錯。

全民英檢中高級線上練習題庫資源成績查詢中級練習初級練習

官方題型說明 · 第二部分 對話

初試聽力共 3 部分 40 題、約 35 分鐘，每題四選一，每題只播出一次。九段對話，每段後面有一到兩個問題，共 15 題。先快速看過選項，聽的時候注意說話者的態度、決定和原因。

第 1、2 題0:31
看逐字稿（先自己聽完再打開）

Questions 1 and 2.

ManHave you decided which university you're applying to?

WomanI'm torn between National Taiwan University and a school in Canada. NTU is closer to home and much cheaper, but studying abroad would really improve my English.

ManWhat do your parents think?

WomanThey'd prefer I stay in Taiwan, but they said it's my decision in the end.

Question one: What is the woman trying to decide?

Question two: What do her parents want?

1.

A：✗ 她沒有提到科系。

B：✗ 她在選學校，不是在考慮工作。

C：✗ 加拿大是學校的選項之一，不是旅行。

D：✓ torn between NTU and a school in Canada：在兩所大學之間猶豫。

2.

A：✗ 父母比較希望她留在台灣。

B：✗ 他們有意見，只是讓她自己決定——『讓她決定』不等於『不在乎』。

C：✓ They'd prefer I stay in Taiwan。

D：✗ 對話沒提到先工作。

第 3 題0:13
看逐字稿（先自己聽完再打開）

Question number three.

WomanThe printer on our floor is jammed again.

ManI'll call the IT department. In the meantime, you can use the one in the meeting room. It's working fine.

Question: What does the man suggest?

3.

A：✗ 他要打給資訊部門處理。

B：✓ 先用會議室那台印表機。

C：✗ 沒有提到買新的。

D：✗ 會議室那台現在就能用。

第 4、5 題0:27
看逐字稿（先自己聽完再打開）

Questions 4 and 5.

ManExcuse me, I ordered the vegetarian set about forty minutes ago, and it still hasn't arrived.

WomanI'm terribly sorry, sir. The kitchen is extremely busy tonight. Let me check on your order right away, and your dessert will be free of charge.

ManThank you. I appreciate it.

Question four: What is the man's complaint?

Question five: What does the woman offer?

4.

A：✗ 餐還沒來，不會冷。

B：✗ 還沒收到任何菜。

C：✓ 等了四十分鐘還沒上菜。

D：✗ 還沒結帳。

5.

A：✓ your dessert will be free of charge：甜點免費。

B：✗ 主餐沒有打折。

C：✗ 沒提到換位子。

D：✗ 沒提到飲料。

第 6、7 題0:31
看逐字稿（先自己聽完再打開）

Questions 6 and 7.

WomanI heard the city is going to turn the old train station into a library and art space.

ManThat's great news. It's been empty for nearly ten years.

WomanSome people want it torn down to build apartments, though. They say the city needs more housing.

ManI understand that, but the building has a lot of history. I'd hate to lose it.

Question six: What will happen to the old train station?

Question seven: What is the man's opinion?

6.

A：✓ 市政府計畫把它改成圖書館和藝術空間。

B：✗ 拆掉蓋公寓是『有些人』的主張，不是決定。

C：✗ 沒有要恢復成車站。

D：✗ 同第二項，是部分人的意見。

7.

A：✗ 他理解這個說法，但不同意。

B：✗ 沒有這樣說。

C：✗ 他說這是好消息。

D：✓ I'd hate to lose it：他希望保留這棟有歷史的建築。

第 8 題0:17
看逐字稿（先自己聽完再打開）

Question number eight.

ManDid you watch the game last night?

WomanOnly the first half. I fell asleep before the end. Who won?

ManThe Lions, but only because of a lucky goal in the last minute.

Question: Why doesn't the woman know the result?

8.

A：✗ 沒有提到加班。

B：✗ 比賽有進行，她看了上半場。

C：✓ I fell asleep before the end。

D：✗ 沒有提到電視。

第 9、10 題0:27
看逐字稿（先自己聽完再打開）

Questions 9 and 10.

WomanI'm thinking about adopting a dog from the animal shelter.

ManThat's wonderful, but have you thought about who will walk it while you're at work?

WomanMy neighbor's son said he'd do it after school for a small fee. And I work from home on Fridays.

ManSounds like you've planned it well.

Question nine: What is the man worried about?

Question ten: How will the woman solve the problem?

9.

A：✓ who will walk it while you're at work。

B：✗ 沒有提到費用問題（fee 是付給鄰居的）。

C：✗ 沒有提到房子大小。

D：✗ 沒有提到狗的健康。

10.

A：✓ 鄰居的兒子放學後幫忙遛狗。

B：✗ 她只是星期五在家工作。

C：✗ 沒提到帶狗上班。

D：✗ 幫忙的是鄰居的兒子，不是她哥哥。

第 11、12 題0:24
看逐字稿（先自己聽完再打開）

Questions 11 and 12.

ManHow did your job interview go?

WomanBetter than I expected. They asked me to give a short presentation in English, which made me nervous, but I think I did well.

ManWhen will you hear back?

WomanThey said by the end of next week.

Question eleven: What made the woman nervous?

Question twelve: When will she get an answer?

11.

A：✗ 沒提到等很久。

B：✗ 沒提到經理。

C：✗ 沒提到薪水。

D：✓ a short presentation in English, which made me nervous。

12.

A：✗ 沒提到明天。

B：✗ 是下週，不是這週。

C：✓ by the end of next week。

D：✗ 沒提到一個月。

第 13 題0:16
看逐字稿（先自己聽完再打開）

Question number thirteen.

WomanCould you pick up my suit from the dry cleaner's on your way home?

ManSure, but I don't have the ticket.

WomanOh, it's under my name—Chen. They'll find it.

Question: What does the man need in order to pick up the suit?

13.

A：✗ 他沒有收據，但女生說不需要。

B：✓ it's under my name：報她的名字就能領。

C：✗ 沒提到付錢。

D：✗ 沒提到信用卡。

第 14、15 題0:31
看逐字稿（先自己聽完再打開）

Questions 14 and 15.

ManThe traffic on the highway is terrible. We'll never make our flight at this rate.

WomanLet's get off at the next exit and take the airport MRT instead. It only takes about thirty-five minutes from the city.

ManGood idea. We can leave the car at the station parking lot.

Question fourteen: What problem do the speakers have?

Question fifteen: What will they probably do?

14.

A：✓ We'll never make our flight at this rate：照這樣可能趕不上飛機。

B：✗ 還沒錯過。

C：✗ 問題是塞車，不是車壞。

D：✗ 他們知道怎麼去機場。

15.

A：✗ 他們要從下一個出口下交流道。

B：✗ 沒提到計程車。

C：✓ take the airport MRT instead，車停在車站停車場。

D：✗ 沒提到改機票。
