英檢中高級口說題目
回答問題・看圖敘述・申述

依全民英檢中高級複試口說的格式出題，共 10 題。聽題、錄音、對照參考回答——在家就能把整個口說流程走一遍。

全民英檢中高級線上練習題庫資源成績查詢中級練習初級練習

官方題型說明

複試口說約 15 分鐘，在語言教室戴耳機、對著麥克風錄音。3 個部分都在這一頁。每題都可以錄下自己的回答再和參考回答比較——錄音只存在你的瀏覽器，不會上傳。

第一部分　回答問題八題，每題播兩次。前四題 15 秒、後四題 30 秒回答。後四題是意見題，要有立場、理由和例子。

1.
第 1 題0:04
看逐字稿（先自己聽完再打開）

ManWhat do you usually do to relax after a busy day?

參考回答：I usually take a hot shower and listen to some music. Sometimes I go for a short walk in the park near my home.

💡 15 秒：一個主要做法＋一個補充。

2.
第 2 題0:04
看逐字稿（先自己聽完再打開）

WomanWhat kind of movies do you enjoy most?

參考回答：I enjoy science fiction movies the most, because they make me imagine what the future might look like.

💡 類型＋原因。

3.
第 3 題0:04
看逐字稿（先自己聽完再打開）

ManHow has your neighborhood changed in the past few years?

參考回答：A new MRT station opened near my home, so there are many more cafés and shops now, but it's also much busier.

💡 用現在完成式或過去式描述變化，最好有正反兩面。

4.
第 4 題0:04
看逐字稿（先自己聽完再打開）

WomanDo you prefer shopping online or in stores?

參考回答：I prefer shopping online because it saves time, but I still buy shoes in stores because I need to try them on.

💡 選擇＋理由＋例外，顯示思考。

5.
第 5 題0:06
看逐字稿（先自己聽完再打開）

ManSome people say social media makes us lonelier. Do you agree? Why or why not?

參考回答：I partly agree. Social media helps me keep in touch with friends who live far away, but when people spend hours scrolling instead of meeting face to face, they can feel more alone. I think the key is to use it to plan real meetings, not to replace them.

💡 30 秒的意見題：表態（可以部分同意）→ 正面 → 負面 → 結論。

6.
第 6 題0:05
看逐字稿（先自己聽完再打開）

WomanIf you could change one thing about your school or workplace, what would it be?

參考回答：I would make lunch breaks longer. Right now we only have forty minutes, which isn't enough time to eat and rest. A longer break would help everyone focus better in the afternoon.

💡 假設語氣 If I could…, I would…，再說明原因和好處。

7.
第 7 題0:05
看逐字稿（先自己聽完再打開）

ManDescribe a person who has influenced you, and explain how.

參考回答：My high school English teacher, Ms. Lin, influenced me a lot. She encouraged us to read English novels for fun instead of just memorizing vocabulary. Because of her, I started reading every day, and now English is my favorite subject.

💡 描述人物：是誰 → 做了什麼 → 對你的具體影響。

8.
第 8 題0:07
看逐字稿（先自己聽完再打開）

WomanMany cities are trying to reduce the number of cars on the road. What do you think is the best way to do this?

參考回答：I think the best way is to make public transportation cheaper and more convenient. If buses and the MRT run more often and cost less, people will leave their cars at home. Cities could also build more safe bike lanes.

💡 提出主要方法＋理由＋第二個方法。

第二部分　看圖敘述30 秒思考、1.5 分鐘描述。除了描述，還要『推測』（probably、might）並連結自己的經驗。

1.
  • Where is this, and what time of day might it be? 這是哪裡？可能是什麼時候？
  • What are the people doing? 圖中的人在做什麼？
  • Why do you think the young man is standing up? 你認為那位年輕人為什麼站起來？
  • Describe your own experience with crowded public transportation. 談談你搭乘擁擠大眾運輸的經驗。
參考回答：This picture shows the inside of an MRT train, probably during the morning rush hour, because it's very crowded and most people are standing. In the middle, a young man is standing up to give his seat to a pregnant woman, which is a kind thing to do. Next to them, a student is reading—maybe he has a test later today. Behind them, other passengers are holding on to the hand straps, and nobody seems to have much space. I take the MRT to school every day, and it's often this crowded. I usually stand near the door so that I can get off quickly. Although it can be uncomfortable, I think most passengers in Taipei are polite, just like the young man in this picture.

💡 看圖敘述要『推測』，不只描述：probably、might、maybe 會讓回答更有深度。依引導問題的順序講，最後連結自己的經驗。

第三部分　申述看一個議題，1.5 分鐘準備（可以寫筆記），再用 1.5 分鐘發表看法。結構：立場 → 兩個理由加例子 → 顧慮 → 結論。

1.
Some people think that students should be required to learn a second foreign language in addition to English. Do you agree? Explain your opinion and give reasons.
有人認為學生除了英文之外，應該被要求學習第二外語。你同意嗎？請說明你的看法並提出理由。
參考回答：I agree that students should learn a second foreign language, but I don't think it should be required for everyone. First, a second language opens doors. Japanese or Korean, for example, can help students understand the music and culture they already enjoy, and it can be useful for future jobs in tourism or trade. Second, learning another language makes students more open-minded, because they see that there is more than one way to express an idea. However, some students are still struggling with English, and adding another language might make them feel overwhelmed. That's why I think schools should offer second languages as optional classes rather than as a requirement. In short, the choice should be available to everyone, but it should be a choice.

💡 1.5 分鐘的申述：開頭表態 → First / Second 兩個理由各附例子 → However 提出顧慮 → In short 結論。準備時間只寫關鍵字，不要寫整句。
