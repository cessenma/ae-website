全民英檢中級聽力題目
第三部分　簡短對話

依全民英檢中級聽力測驗格式出題，共 10 題。做完按「對答案」，每一題都有中文解析：為什麼對、為什麼錯。

全民英檢中級中級總覽線上練習題庫資源成績查詢初級練習

官方題型說明 · 第三部分 簡短對話

初試聽力共 4 部分 35 題、約 30 分鐘，每題四選一，每題只播出一次。兩人對話加一個問題、四個選項。常見陷阱是對話中出現的數字、時間被『改掉』或『混搭』，答案要以最後確定的資訊為準。

第 1 題0:16
看逐字稿（先自己聽完再打開）

ManExcuse me, is this seat taken?

WomanNo, but my friend is coming back in a minute. The one by the window is free, though.

ManOh, thanks. I'll sit there.

Question: Where will the man probably sit?

第 1 題

A：✓ 女生說靠窗的位子是空的，男生說 I'll sit there。

B：✗ 女生旁邊的位子是留給朋友的。

C：✗ 朋友馬上會回來，不能坐。

D：✗ 對話沒提到門邊。

第 2 題0:15
看逐字稿（先自己聽完再打開）

WomanDid you remember to buy milk on your way home?

ManOh no, I forgot. I'll go to the convenience store after dinner.

WomanCould you get some eggs, too? We're almost out.

Question: What will the man buy after dinner?

第 2 題

A：✗ 女生又請他買蛋。

B：✗ 牛奶是他本來就要買的。

C：✓ 本來要買牛奶，女生又加了蛋：兩樣都要買。

D：✗ 沒有提到麵包。

第 3 題0:14
看逐字稿（先自己聽完再打開）

ManI heard you're moving to Taichung.

WomanYes, my father got a new job there. We're leaving next month, but I'll come back to visit during summer vacation.

Question: Why is the woman's family moving?

第 3 題

A：✓ my father got a new job there：爸爸在台中找到新工作。

B：✗ 沒有提到轉學。

C：✗ 沒有提到房租。

D：✗ 沒有提到祖父母。

第 4 題0:14
看逐字稿（先自己聽完再打開）

WomanHow was the concert last night?

ManThe band was great, but we had to stand for three hours. My legs still hurt!

WomanNext time, buy seat tickets.

Question: What was the problem with the concert?

第 4 題

A：✗ 他說樂團很棒。

B：✗ 沒有提到票價。

C：✓ 他們站了三小時，女生建議下次買座位票：沒有座位。

D：✗ 沒有提到開始時間。

第 5 題0:15
看逐字稿（先自己聽完再打開）

ManHello, I'd like to change my dentist appointment on Thursday.

WomanSure. We have openings on Friday at two or Saturday at ten.

ManSaturday morning is better for me.

Question: When will the man see the dentist?

第 5 題

A：✗ 星期四是原本要改掉的時間。

B：✗ 他選的是星期六。

C：✗ 星期六的空檔是十點，不是兩點——把兩個時間混在一起是陷阱。

D：✓ Saturday morning＝星期六早上十點。

第 6 題0:17
看逐字稿（先自己聽完再打開）

WomanThis jacket looks nice on you, but it's a little big.

ManDo you have it in a smaller size?

WomanSorry, we only have this color in large. How about the black one?

ManOK, I'll try it on.

Question: What will the man do next?

第 6 題

A：✗ 大號的太大了。

B：✓ 店員推薦黑色那件，他說 I'll try it on。

C：✗ 沒有說要去別家店。

D：✗ 沒有提到折扣。

第 7 題0:18
看逐字稿（先自己聽完再打開）

ManMom, can I go to the movies with Jeff tonight?

WomanHave you finished your homework?

ManAlmost. I just have one more math problem.

WomanFinish it first, and be home by ten.

Question: What does the mother want the boy to do before he goes out?

第 7 題

A：✗ 沒有提到打掃。

B：✗ 沒有要他打電話。

C：✓ Finish it first＝先把最後一題數學寫完。

D：✗ 沒有提到買票。

第 8 題0:15
看逐字稿（先自己聽完再打開）

WomanThe typhoon is coming tomorrow. Do you think school will be canceled?

ManThe news said the city will decide at eight tonight.

WomanI hope we get a day off!

Question: When will they know if there is school tomorrow?

第 8 題

A：✓ 市政府今晚八點會決定。

B：✗ 是今晚，不是明天早上。

C：✗ 颱風過後才知道就太晚了。

D：✗ 現在還不知道，所以她才說希望放假。

第 9 題0:16
看逐字稿（先自己聽完再打開）

ManI'm so tired of eating at the night market every weekend.

WomanThen let's cook at home this Saturday. I can make my grandmother's beef noodle soup.

ManThat sounds perfect.

Question: What will they probably do on Saturday?

第 9 題

A：✗ 男生說已經吃膩夜市了。

B：✗ 是做奶奶的食譜，不是去拜訪奶奶。

C：✗ 沒有提到餐廳。

D：✓ let's cook at home this Saturday：星期六在家煮。

第 10 題0:20
看逐字稿（先自己聽完再打開）

WomanDid you see the email from Ms. Lin? The field trip has been moved.

ManMoved to where?

WomanNot where—when. It's next Wednesday instead of Tuesday because of the rain.

Question: What changed about the field trip?

第 10 題

A：✗ 女生特別說 Not where：不是地點。

B：✓ Not where—when：改的是日期（星期二改星期三）。

C：✗ 沒有換老師。

D：✗ 沒有提到費用。
