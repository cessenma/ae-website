全民英檢中級 口說練習
朗讀短文・回答問題・看圖敘述

依複試口說格式出題，共 13 題。聽題、錄音、對照參考回答——在家就能模擬語言教室的考試流程。

全民英檢中級中級總覽線上練習題庫資源成績查詢初級練習

官方題型說明

複試口說約 15 分鐘，在語言教室戴耳機、對著麥克風錄音。三個部分都在這一頁。每題都可以錄下自己的回答再和參考回答比較——錄音只存在你的瀏覽器，不會上傳。

第一部分　朗讀短文兩篇短文，先默讀一分鐘，每篇有兩分鐘唸出來。看的是發音、重音、語調與停頓。
1.

Many people in Taiwan ride scooters to work every day. Scooters are cheap, fast, and easy to park, but they can also be dangerous. Every year, thousands of riders are hurt in accidents. To stay safe, riders should always wear a helmet, follow the speed limit, and never use their phones while riding. Being careful for a few seconds can save a life.

💡 scooters、helmet、accidents 的重音要正確；逗號小停、句號停久一點；最後一句放慢，語氣加重。

2.

Welcome to the Sunshine Science Museum. On the first floor, you can learn how weather works and even stand inside a small typhoon machine. The second floor is all about space, with a model of the moon that visitors can touch. If you get hungry, our café on the third floor serves sandwiches, drinks, and ice cream. Enjoy your visit!

💡 導覽的語氣要親切；typhoon 重音在第二音節 ty-PHOON；café 唸 ca-FÉ；列舉 sandwiches, drinks, and ice cream 前兩項上揚、最後一項下降。

第二部分　回答問題十題，每題播兩次。前五題有 15 秒、後五題有 30 秒回答。後五題要有結構：立場＋理由＋例子。

Number one.

ManWhat time do you usually go to bed on school nights?

參考回答：I usually go to bed at about eleven, because I often finish my homework late.

💡 回答時間後補一句原因，比只說 eleven 好很多。

參考回答：I would tell my friend to start slowly, for example by walking for thirty minutes after dinner. It's also a good idea to find a partner, because exercising together is more fun and you won't give up easily.

第三部分　看圖敘述看一張圖 30 秒，再用 1.5 分鐘描述。照著試題上的引導問題說：地點、人物在做什麼、自己的經驗。
1.
  • Where are these people? 這是什麼地方？
  • What are the people doing? 圖片中的人在做什麼？
  • Have you been to a place like this? What did you do there? 你去過類似的地方嗎？
  • If you still have time, describe other details in the picture. 如果還有時間，請描述圖中其他細節。
參考回答：This picture shows a busy night market in Taiwan. It's evening, and there are a lot of lanterns and food stalls. On the left, a man is cooking meat on a grill, and the smoke is going up into the air. In the middle, a girl is buying a cup of bubble tea. A family of four is sitting at a small table and eating together, and they look very happy. Above them, there are many round paper lanterns, so the whole street is bright. I have been to Raohe Night Market with my friends. We tried stinky tofu and drank bubble tea, just like the people in this picture. Night markets are noisy, but they are one of my favorite places in Taiwan.

💡 看圖敘述的順序：地點與時間 → 由左到右描述人物和動作（用現在進行式）→ 連結自己的經驗 → 一句感想。1.5 分鐘大約要說 8–10 句。
