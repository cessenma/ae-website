英檢中級寫作範例
英文作文

依全民英檢中級寫作測驗格式出題。先自己寫，再打開範文，對照每一句的解析。

全民英檢中級中級總覽線上練習題庫資源成績查詢初級練習

官方題型說明 · 第二部分 英文作文

複試寫作 40 分鐘：中譯英（40%）＋ 英文作文約 120 字（60%）。依題目寫約 120 字的短文。評分看：立場清楚、理由有例子、段落有連接詞、文法用字正確。

你認為學生應該被允許在學校使用手機嗎？請寫一篇約 120 字的英文短文，說明你的看法和理由。
Do you think students should be allowed to use cell phones at school? Write about 120 words. Give your opinion and your reasons.

0 字

範文（121 字）

I believe students should be allowed to use cell phones at school, but only with clear rules. First, phones are useful learning tools. Students can look up new words, check information for projects, and use learning apps in class. Second, phones help students stay safe. If a student feels sick or misses the bus, he or she can contact parents right away. However, phones can also be a problem. Some students play games or chat online instead of listening to the teacher. Therefore, schools should let students use phones only during breaks or when teachers ask them to. In my opinion, with good rules, cell phones can help students more than they hurt them.

範文為什麼拿高分：逐句解析

  • I believe students should be allowed to use cell phones at school, but only with clear rules.
    第一句就表明立場，閱卷老師一眼看到你的看法。
  • First, phones are useful learning tools.
    First 開啟第一個理由，段落有結構。
  • Students can look up new words, check information for projects, and use learning apps in class.
    用三個具體例子支持理由，而不是空泛地說『很方便』。
  • Second, phones help students stay safe.
    Second 帶出第二個理由。
  • If a student feels sick or misses the bus, he or she can contact parents right away.
    if 條件句給出情境，內容更有說服力。
  • However, phones can also be a problem.
    However 呈現另一面的看法，顯示思考完整。
  • Some students play games or chat online instead of listening to the teacher.
    instead of＋V-ing 的正確用法。
  • Therefore, schools should let students use phones only during breaks or when teachers ask them to.
    Therefore 提出解決方法，呼應開頭的『有規則』。
  • In my opinion, with good rules, cell phones can help students more than they hurt them.
    結論句再次回到立場，首尾呼應。

常見錯誤

  • 只寫立場、沒有理由或例子。
  • 每句都用 I think 開頭，句型單調。
  • 字數明顯不足 120 字。
  • 把 advice / information 加 s。

交卷前自我檢查

  • 第一句清楚表明立場？
  • 至少兩個理由，每個理由有例子？
  • 有用 First / Second / However / Therefore 連接？
  • 結論句回應立場？
  • 約 120 字？

常見問題

英檢中級寫作常見問題

複試寫作 40 分鐘，分兩題：中譯英（把一段中文翻成英文）和英文作文（約 120 字）。

寫作 80 分通過（滿分 100 分）。中譯英佔 40%、英文作文佔 60%，兩題都以 0–5 級分評定後換算成百分制。

由兩位受過訓練的合格評分老師分別評閱（初審與複審）；如果兩人的分數相差超過一個級分，會再由資深評分老師評閱。
