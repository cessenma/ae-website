小學英檢聽力題目
第四部分　選擇題

依小學英檢聽力測驗格式出題，共 7 題。做完按「對答案」，每一題都有中文解析：為什麼對、為什麼錯。

小學英檢小學英檢總覽線上練習初級練習題庫資源

官方題型說明 · 第四部分 選擇題

筆試的聽力共 4 部分 25 題、約 20 分鐘，每題播放兩次，發音以美式為主。先聽到、也看到題目，再聽一段對話，三選一。先想清楚題目問的是 who、what、where、when 還是 how。

第 1 題0:21
看逐字稿（先自己聽完再打開）

What does the boy want to eat?

ManMom, I'm hungry.

WomanDo you want a sandwich?

ManNo, thanks. Can I have some noodles?

WomanOK.

Listen again.

What does the boy want to eat?

ManMom, I'm hungry.

WomanDo you want a sandwich?

ManNo, thanks. Can I have some noodles?

WomanOK.

What does the boy want to eat?

A：✗ 對話沒有提到飯。

B：✓ Can I have some noodles? 他想吃麵。

C：✗ 媽媽問要不要三明治，他說 No, thanks。

第 2 題0:19
看逐字稿（先自己聽完再打開）

Where is Grandma?

WomanWhere's Grandma?

ManShe's not in the kitchen. She's in the garden.

WomanLet's go and see her.

Listen again.

Where is Grandma?

WomanWhere's Grandma?

ManShe's not in the kitchen. She's in the garden.

WomanLet's go and see her.

Where is Grandma?

A：✓ She's in the garden. 奶奶在花園。

B：✗ She's not in the kitchen——not 表示『不在』廚房。

C：✗ 沒有提到臥室。

第 3 題0:17
看逐字稿（先自己聽完再打開）

How old is Kevin's sister?

WomanKevin, how old is your sister?

ManShe's six. I'm ten.

Listen again.

How old is Kevin's sister?

WomanKevin, how old is your sister?

ManShe's six. I'm ten.

How old is Kevin's sister?

A：✓ 妹妹六歲：She's six.

B：✗ 十歲是 Kevin 自己。

C：✗ sixteen 和 six 聽起來像，但沒有這個數字。

第 4 題0:27
看逐字稿（先自己聽完再打開）

What will the girl do after school?

ManLet's play basketball after school.

WomanSorry, I can't. I have piano class.

ManOK. Then let's play tomorrow.

WomanGreat!

Listen again.

What will the girl do after school?

ManLet's play basketball after school.

WomanSorry, I can't. I have piano class.

ManOK. Then let's play tomorrow.

WomanGreat!

What will the girl do after school?

A：✗ 她說 Sorry, I can't.，今天不能打球。

B：✓ I have piano class. 她要上鋼琴課。

C：✗ 沒有提到回家睡覺。

第 5 題0:20
看逐字稿（先自己聽完再打開）

What time is it now?

WomanHurry up! It's seven thirty.

ManOh no! The bus comes at seven forty.

Listen again.

What time is it now?

WomanHurry up! It's seven thirty.

ManOh no! The bus comes at seven forty.

What time is it now?

A：✗ 沒有提到七點整。

B：✗ 七點四十是公車來的時間。

C：✓ It's seven thirty. 現在七點半。

第 6 題0:15
看逐字稿（先自己聽完再打開）

What's the weather like today?

ManCan we go to the beach today?

WomanNo, it's raining. Let's watch a movie at home.

Listen again.

What's the weather like today?

ManCan we go to the beach today?

WomanNo, it's raining. Let's watch a movie at home.

What's the weather like today?

A：✗ 如果是晴天就可以去海邊了。

B：✗ 沒有提到風。

C：✓ it's raining：下雨天。

第 7 題0:22
看逐字稿（先自己聽完再打開）

What color is Mary's new bag?

WomanLook! This is my new bag.

ManWow, it's pink. I like it.

WomanThank you.

Listen again.

What color is Mary's new bag?

WomanLook! This is my new bag.

ManWow, it's pink. I like it.

WomanThank you.

What color is Mary's new bag?

A：✓ it's pink：粉紅色。

B：✗ 沒有提到藍色。

C：✗ 沒有提到黑色。
