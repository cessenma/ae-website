官方題型說明 Task format

六則生活短文（告示、簡訊、標籤），每則一題三選一，考「主要訊息」。官方考試中為第 1–6 題，共 6 分。陷阱通常是「有寫到但不是重點」。

題目說明（rubric）依照官方格式以英文呈現；以下練習題為埃森美語原創編寫，用於熟悉題型，非劍橋官方試題。官方免費樣卷：cambridgeenglish.org → A2 Key for Schools preparation。

For each question, choose the correct answer.

(1) Notice

SCHOOL NOTICE

There is no PE class this Wednesday. Please bring your art things instead. It may rain, so bring an umbrella too.
— Miss Wang

(2) Sign

NEW CITY POOL

Children under 10 years old must swim with an adult. No food or drinks beside the pool. Please shower before you get in the water.

(3) Message

Amy: Hi Tina! Sorry, I can't meet at 4 today. My mom needs me at home. Can we meet at 5 instead? See you at the MRT station, not the bookstore this time.

(4) Email

Subject: Field Trip Money

Hi parents,
Please send NT$150 for the museum trip by this Friday. Send the money in an envelope with your child's name on it. Thank you!
— Mr. Lee

(5) Label

ISLAND APPLE JUICE

100% real fruit juice — no sugar added.
Keep in the fridge after opening.
Drink within 3 days.

(6) Announcement

SUNSHINE SWIM CLUB

Our club is moving to a new pool on Zhongshan Road. Classes stop this Saturday and start again on Monday at the new place. Same teachers, same times — just a new address!

解析會說明每個選項為什麼對、為什麼錯——這是課堂上老師做的事，我們把它放上來。分數只記在你自己的瀏覽器裡，不會上傳。

練習之後

短文題最簡單，
但看懂單字 ≠ 讀出目的

Part 1 的六篇短文乍看都很簡單，很多孩子每個單字都認得，卻還是選錯答案——因為看懂單字，不代表讀出了這則訊息真正的目的。誰寫的、寫給誰看、想請對方做什麼，這種「目的感」沒辦法靠背單字練出來，要靠大量真實情境的英文互動慢慢養成。想知道孩子的閱讀理解卡在哪個環節，歡迎加 LINE 預約免費程度診斷。
