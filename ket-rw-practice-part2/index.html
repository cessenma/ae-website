題目說明（rubric）依照官方格式以英文呈現；以下練習題為埃森美語原創編寫，用於熟悉題型，非劍橋官方試題。官方免費樣卷：cambridgeenglish.org → A2 Key for Schools preparation。

For each question, choose the correct answer. Read the descriptions of the three food stalls and the seven sentences about what people want. Decide which food stall (A, B or C) is best for each person.

A — Lucky Noodle Stall

Lucky Noodle Stall stands by the market gate. Mr. Chen's noodle soup is hot, and there's always a vegetarian bowl for people who don't eat meat. There are ten tables, so families can sit down together, and small bowls are ready for young children. Open every evening, 5 pm to 11 pm. A bowl costs NT$60.

B — Sweet Moon Drinks

Sweet Moon Drinks sells iced bubble tea and fruit juice — perfect on a hot night. There are no seats; people take their drinks and walk around the market. Prices start at NT$40, and you can ask for less sugar or ice. This stall stays open until midnight, later than the others. Pay by cash or mobile phone.

C — Golden Dumplings

Golden Dumplings cooks pork dumplings right in front of you, so you can watch them fry or steam. There are a few stools, but most people eat standing up as they explore the market. Ten dumplings cost just NT$50 — a great price for a quick snack. Open 6 pm to 10 pm; closed on Mondays.

解析會說明每個選項為什麼對、為什麼錯——這是課堂上老師做的事，我們把它放上來。分數只記在你自己的瀏覽器裡，不會上傳。
