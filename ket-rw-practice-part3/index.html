官方題型說明 Task format

一篇約 230 字的長文，5 題三選一。官方考試中為第 15–19 題。考細節理解與主旨——選項常常「文中有寫但答非所問」，這正是解析要教的。

題目說明（rubric）依照官方格式以英文呈現；以下練習題為埃森美語原創編寫，用於熟悉題型，非劍橋官方試題。官方免費樣卷：cambridgeenglish.org → A2 Key for Schools preparation。

Amy writes a blog post about joining her school's swimming team. Read the article and then answer the questions. For each question, choose the correct answer, A, B or C.

Joining the Swimming Team

My name is Amy and I am twelve years old. Last September, I joined my school's swimming team. I was nervous because I could only swim slowly, and some of the other students were very fast. On the first day, the coach, Mr. Lin, put me in the beginner group. We trained every Tuesday and Thursday morning, from 6:30 to 7:30, before school started. At first, I hated waking up so early, and the pool water always felt cold.

After two weeks, I wanted to stop. But my older sister, who was also on the team two years ago, told me to keep trying. She said swimming gets easier if you practice every week. So I didn't stop. Mr. Lin also helped me a lot, and slowly my swimming got faster.

In November, our team had a small race against another school. I didn't win, but I finished in third place in my group. I was so happy and excited! My parents came to watch, and they took a photo of me next to the pool.

Now I train three mornings a week, and I don't mind the early starts anymore. I have also made some new friends on the team. Next year, I want to try a longer race. Joining the team was hard at the beginning, but I am very glad I did it.

解析會說明每個選項為什麼對、為什麼錯——這是課堂上老師做的事，我們把它放上來。分數只記在你自己的瀏覽器裡，不會上傳。

長文閱讀，
錯的不是單字，是選項換句話說

Part 3 長文題錯最多的，往往不是單字量不夠，而是看不出「選項換句話說」的陷阱——三個選項常常都在文章裡找得到影子，只有一個真正回答了問題。這種辨識能力，靠的是大量共讀和課堂討論慢慢養成；這份題庫能幫孩子熟悉題型、驗收有沒有抓到陷阱邏輯，但沒辦法取代平常的閱讀累積。
