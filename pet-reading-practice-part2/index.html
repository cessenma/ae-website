官方題型說明 Task format

五個人各有自己的需求，八則廣告選五個最合適的。多出來的三則是干擾選項——每一個人的敘述裡都有三到四個條件，全部要滿足才算對。官方考卷中為 第 6–10 題。

題目說明（rubric）依照官方題型格式以英文呈現；以下練習題為埃森美語原創編寫，用於熟悉題型，非劍橋官方試題。官方免費樣卷：cambridgeenglish.org → B1 Preliminary for Schools preparation。

For each question, choose the correct answer.

Code Your Own App

CODE YOUR OWN APP — FREE ONLINE CAMP
Ever wanted to build your own app? This free online course runs every weekday morning for two weeks in July. No experience needed — just a laptop and an internet connection. Open to students aged 13–16. Live classes are recorded, so you can watch again if you miss one.

Helping Hands at the Night Market

HELPING HANDS AT THE NIGHT MARKET
Spend your weekends helping elderly sellers at Banqiao Night Market: carrying boxes, taking photos of their food for social media, or just chatting with customers. It's unpaid, but sellers often give you a free snack to say thank you! Volunteers must be at least 14 and enjoy talking to new people.

Yangmingshan Hiking Club

YANGMINGSHAN HIKING CLUB
Join us every Saturday for a walk in Yangmingshan National Park. Routes are chosen for beginners, so you don't need to be very fit — just keen to be outdoors. There's a small fee (NT$100) to cover the shared minibus. Open to anyone aged 12 and over; a parent or guardian must sign a permission form.

Summer Strings Course, Austria

SUMMER STRINGS COURSE, AUSTRIA
A two-week course for advanced young musicians aged 15–18 who already play really well. You must apply with a recording of yourself playing, and the course is not cheap — but students who go say the chance to practise with other serious players is worth every cent.

Drop-in Language Exchange Cafe

DROP-IN LANGUAGE EXCHANGE CAFE
Practise your spoken English with friendly volunteers from the UK, the US and Australia. No booking, no fee, and no need to come every week — just drop in on Tuesday or Thursday evenings after school and join whichever table has space. All levels and all ages are welcome.

Swimming Improvers Course

SWIMMING IMPROVERS COURSE
Already comfortable in the water but want a better stroke? This six-week course at the community pool focuses on technique, not the basics, so you should already be able to swim a full length. Weekday afternoon classes, small fee, ages 10–15.

Poster & Illustration Workshop

POSTER & ILLUSTRATION WORKSHOP
Design your own posters and characters at this relaxed weekend workshop at the community centre. No previous art experience is required, just an interest in drawing. There's a small materials fee, and the workshop is open to anyone aged 13 or older.

Part-time Help Wanted — Campus Bookstore

PART-TIME HELP WANTED — CAMPUS BOOKSTORE
Earn some pocket money this summer! We need a friendly weekend assistant to help customers and keep the shelves tidy. You must be at least 16 and enjoy talking to people. Training is provided, so no experience is necessary — just bring a positive attitude.

1. Ben is thirteen and wants to spend part of his summer learning to build an app. His family live outside the city, so he needs something he can join from home, and he does not have money to spend on a course.

A：✓ 正確答案：'This free online course' 對應 Ben 沒有預算，'aged 13–16' 符合他的年齡，而且是線上課程，符合他住在市外、需要在家上課的需求。

B：✗ 陷阱：此活動免費，但內容是到夜市幫忙擺攤，不是學寫程式，主題不符 Ben 的需求。

C：✗ 陷阱：登山社每週六在陽明山現場集合，Ben 需要能在家參加的活動，而且與寫程式無關。

D：✗ 陷阱：此課程需要試奏錄音甄選且費用高昂，與 Ben 想免費學程式完全無關。

E：✗ 陷阱：語言交換咖啡是練英文口說，不是學寫程式，主題不符。

F：✗ 陷阱：游泳課需要到社區泳池現場上課，且與寫程式無關。

G：✗ 陷阱：海報插畫工作坊是週末到社區中心上課，Ben 需要能在家參加的線上活動，此項不符。

H：✗ 陷阱：書店打工要求年滿16歲且需到現場工作，Ben 才13歲，也非學程式。

2. Grace wants to get some exercise outdoors this summer. She is busy with cram school on weekdays, so it has to be a weekend activity, and as she is not very fit yet, she would like something suitable for beginners.

A：✗ 陷阱：程式營是平日早上的線上課程，既不是戶外活動，時間也與 Grace 補習衝突。

B：✗ 陷阱：夜市志工雖然是週末活動，但內容是幫忙擺攤，不是戶外運動。

C：✓ 正確答案：'every Saturday' 符合她只能週末參加，'Routes are chosen for beginners' 符合她還不太健康、需要新手路線，'NT$100' 費用也不高。

D：✗ 陷阱：音樂營需要高超琴藝與高額費用，與 Grace 想要戶外運動的需求完全無關。

E：✗ 陷阱：語言交換是平日晚上的室內活動，不是戶外運動。

F：✗ 陷阱：游泳課是平日下午上課，與 Grace 只能週末參加的條件不符。

G：✗ 陷阱：海報工作坊雖然是週末活動，但是室內畫畫課程，不是戶外運動。

H：✗ 陷阱：書店打工雖然是週末工作，但是室內工作，不是戶外運動。

3. Iris has played the violin for eight years and is looking for a serious challenge alongside other advanced young musicians. Her parents are happy to pay for a good course, even if it is not cheap.

A：✗ 陷阱：程式營與音樂完全無關，不符 Iris 的專長。

B：✗ 陷阱：夜市志工與音樂無關，也不是給程度好的音樂生挑戰的活動。

C：✗ 陷阱：登山社是給新手的健行活動，與 Iris 想要的音樂挑戰無關。

D：✓ 正確答案：'advanced young musicians... who already play really well' 符合 Iris 拉了八年小提琴、程度好的背景，'the course is not cheap' 也符合她家人願意付高額學費。

E：✗ 陷阱：語言交換咖啡是練英文，與音樂無關。

F：✗ 陷阱：游泳課與音樂無關。

G：✗ 陷阱：海報插畫工作坊是給沒有經驗的人上的美術課，不是給程度好的音樂生。

H：✗ 陷阱：書店打工是賺零用錢的工作，與 Iris 想要的音樂挑戰無關，也非高額課程。

4. Tom is sixteen and wants to earn some money before school starts again. He is comfortable talking to customers, but because of family plans he can only work at the weekend.

A：✗ 陷阱：程式營是免費課程，不是打工賺錢，不符 Tom 想賺錢的需求。

B：✗ 陷阱：'It's unpaid' 明確說明夜市志工沒有薪水，不符 Tom 想賺錢的需求，是條件最接近卻被『無薪』排除的陷阱選項。

C：✗ 陷阱：登山社不僅不支薪，還要繳交通費，與 Tom 想賺錢的目標相反。

D：✗ 陷阱：音樂營需要花大錢參加，不是賺錢的工作。

E：✗ 陷阱：語言交換咖啡是免費活動，不是打工。

F：✗ 陷阱：游泳課要繳費上課，不是打工賺錢。

G：✗ 陷阱：海報工作坊要繳材料費上課，不是打工賺錢。

H：✓ 正確答案：'Earn some pocket money' 符合 Tom 想賺錢，'weekend assistant' 符合他只能週末工作，'enjoy talking to people' 符合他習慣與顧客應對。

5. Chloe wants to feel more confident chatting in English with people from other countries. She is usually free on weekday evenings after school, but she does not want to promise to come every single week.

A：✗ 陷阱：程式營雖然是線上課程，但主題是寫程式，不是練英文口說。

B：✗ 陷阱：夜市志工只在週末，與 Chloe 平日晚上有空的條件不符，主題也不是練英文。

C：✗ 陷阱：登山社在週六，與 Chloe 平日晚上有空的時間不符，主題也不是練英文。

D：✗ 陷阱：音樂營與練習英文口說無關。

E：✓ 正確答案：'Practise your spoken English with friendly volunteers from the UK, the US and Australia' 符合她想和外國人練英文，'Tuesday or Thursday evenings' 符合她平日晚上有空，'no need to come every week' 符合她不想每週固定出席。

F：✗ 陷阱：游泳課是平日下午，且與練英文無關。

G：✗ 陷阱：海報工作坊在週末，且是畫畫課，與練英文口說無關。

H：✗ 陷阱：書店打工在週末，且是工作場合而非練英文的活動。
