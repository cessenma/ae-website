官方題型說明 Task format

練習 A　（第 1 組，共 2 組）

Our Class Trip to Sun Moon Lake

Last month, our whole class went (1) a three-day trip to Sun Moon Lake. We left school so early in the morning (2) most of us were still half asleep on the bus. When we arrived, our teacher gave us the choice (3) walking around the lake or hiring bicycles instead. I decided to cycle with my best friend, (4) loves cycling even more than I do. The path around the lake was much (5) beautiful than I had expected, with mountains on one side and clear blue water on the other. By the end of the day, my legs were so tired that I could hardly stand up, but I had (6) better time than on any other school trip.

1. 第 (1) 格

解析：go on a trip/holiday 是固定介系詞搭配，表示「進行一趟旅行」。

2. 第 (2) 格

解析：so + 形容詞/副詞 + that 是「如此…以致於…」的固定句型，表示結果，此處與前面的 so early 對應。

3. 第 (3) 格

解析：the choice of + 動名詞 是固定用法，choice 後面須接介系詞 of 再加 V-ing，表示「…的選擇」。

4. 第 (4) 格

解析：先行詞 my best friend 是人，且逗號後為非限定關係子句，只能用 who，不能用 that。

5. 第 (5) 格

解析：much more + 形容詞 + than 是比較級的固定用法，much 用來加強比較級的語氣。

6. 第 (6) 格

解析：have a good/better time 是固定片語，表示「玩得開心」，此處 better 前需要冠詞 a。

練習 B　（第 2 組，共 2 組）

An Email to Marco

Hi Marco,
Thank you (1) your last email — it was great to hear about your summer camp in Italy! I have some exciting news too: next month I am going to visit my aunt (2) lives in Vancouver, Canada. I (3) never been to Canada before, so I am so excited that I can hardly sleep at night!
My aunt says the mountains near her house are (4) bigger than the ones in the photos she sent me. We are planning to go hiking on (5) of the days, and I am hoping to see a bear from a safe distance!
Please write back soon and tell me (6) your camp went. Did you make any new friends there?
Best wishes,
Amy

1. 第 (1) 格

解析：thank you for + 事物 是感謝某事的固定介系詞搭配。

2. 第 (2) 格

解析：先行詞 my aunt 是人且為限定用法（無逗號），關係代名詞可用 who 或 that。

3. 第 (3) 格

解析：have + never + 過去分詞 been 是現在完成式的固定結構，表示「從未有過的經驗」。

4. 第 (4) 格

解析：even / much / far 都可加強比較級 bigger than 的語氣，表示「甚至、更加…」；但不能用 very 直接修飾比較級。

5. 第 (5) 格

解析：on one of the days 表示「其中一天」，one 在此作代名詞，指 the days 當中的一天。

6. 第 (6) 格

解析：tell me how + 子句 是間接問句的用法，詢問事情「進行得如何」。
