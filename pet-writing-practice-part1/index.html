必考 email

4 個原創題目（20 分・約 100 字）。直接在頁面上寫，字數即時計算，寫完再打開範文對照——每一句都標出它在為哪一個評分面向服務。

官方題型說明 Task format

回一封朋友的 email，四個內容重點全部要寫到，還要有稱呼、署名與合適的語氣。這一題沒得選，一定要寫。這一題官方配分 20 分，字數要求 約 100 字。

題目說明依官方題型格式以英文呈現；以下題目與範文為埃森美語原創編寫，非劍橋官方試題或官方範文。

寫作沒辦法自動改分——所以這一頁的重點不是給你分數，是先自己寫一次再對照。草稿只存在你自己的瀏覽器裡，關掉頁面也還在，不會上傳。

題目 1

Club fundraising

Task 1

This is an email from your English friend, Daniel.
Write an email to Daniel.
In your email, remember to:
- say what your class sold
- say if the fundraising day was successful
- give Daniel one tip for his own fundraiser
- ask Daniel when his fundraiser is
Write about 100 words.

Hi! My cousin goes to a school near yours, and she told me your class had a big fundraising day last Saturday! That sounds like so much fun. What did you sell? I really hope it went well! Also, I'm helping to organise a small fundraiser at my school next month, so any tips would be great. Write soon, Daniel
  • say what your class sold
  • say if the fundraising day was successful
  • give Daniel one tip for his own fundraiser
  • ask Daniel when his fundraiser is

Write about 100 words.

0 字 目標 約 100 字
交卷前先自己檢查
看範文與逐句解析（100 字）
OrganisationHi Daniel,
以朋友的名字開頭稱呼，是 email 該有的格式，屬於「組織」看的基本結構。
CommunicativeThanks for your email; it's great to hear from you!
先回應對方來信、語氣自然親切，讓這封信像在「對話」而不是硬邦邦地報告，這是「溝通達成」要看的地方。
ContentOur class sold bubble tea and handmade bookmarks at the fundraising day.
直接、具體地回答第一個重點——賣了什麼——沒有寫得含糊籠統，「內容」滿分的關鍵就是具體。
ContentIt was really successful because we sold everything before lunchtime and raised over eight thousand dollars for the animal shelter.
清楚回答第二個重點——活動是否成功——並用數字和具體結果（賣光、募到多少錢）證明，讓讀者被「完整告知」。
ContentMy tip for your fundraiser is to choose something cheap to make, so you can earn more money.
直接給 Daniel 一個具體、可執行的建議（成本低才能賺更多），回答第三個重點，不是空泛的加油打氣。
LanguageYou could also ask a teacher for a table near the school gate because more people will see it there.
用 because 子句說明原因，句子結構比簡單句複雜，展現「語言」評分要看的句型廣度，而不只是一堆短句。
ContentWhen is your fundraiser going to be?
用正確的問句形式回答第四個重點——反問 Daniel 的活動時間——這是官方评分表明確要求的「詢問」功能。
CommunicativeI can't wait to hear all about it!
用一句表達期待的話收尾，讓讀者感覺這封信有溫度、有互動，而不是寫完四點就結束。
OrganisationBest wishes, Mia
有署名，讓整封信首尾完整——這也對應到官方檢查表裡「有沒有稱呼和署名」這一項。
最常見的失分
  • 只提到賣了什麼，卻忘記說明活動是否成功（第 2 點），導致 Content 漏掉一個重點。
  • 沒有給 Daniel 具體的建議（第 3 點只寫『it will be good』這種空泛的話），內容不夠具體。
  • 忘記在信末提出問題（第 4 點 ask Daniel when...），整封信少了「詢問」這個功能，Content 和 Communicative Achievement 都會被扣分。
  • 時態混亂——描述已經發生的園遊會卻用現在式（例如寫成 'We sell bubble tea'），Language 會被扣分。
題目 2

School trip planning

Task 1

This is an email from your English friend, Olivia.
Write an email to Olivia.
In your email, remember to:
- say which place your class chose for the school trip
- explain why your class chose that place
- say one thing you plan to do there
- ask Olivia about her last school trip
Write about 100 words.

Hi! How are you? My pen friend in Taipei told me that your class is planning a school trip and you finally chose a place! She said you were choosing between an old street with lots of shops and a science museum. Which place did you pick in the end? I'm really curious! Talk soon, Olivia
  • say which place your class chose
  • explain why your class chose that place
  • say one thing you plan to do there
  • ask Olivia about her last school trip

Write about 100 words.

0 字 目標 約 100 字
交卷前先自己檢查
看範文與逐句解析（99 字）
OrganisationHi Olivia,
稱呼對應朋友的名字，信件格式正確。
CommunicativeIt's lovely to hear from you, and yes, we finally decided!
先回應對方的好奇心（you finally chose a place!），語氣自然，像在跟朋友聊天而不是條列報告。
ContentOur class chose Jiufen Old Street instead of the science museum.
明確回答第一個重點——選了哪裡——並用 instead of 順帶交代另一個選項沒被選中，資訊完整。
LanguageWe picked it because most of us had never been there, and everyone loves trying new street food.
用 because 子句加上並列子句，句子結構有深度，同時也帶到了第二個重點（為什麼選它）的原因說明。
ContentWhen we go, I'm planning to try the famous taro balls and take photos of the mountain views.
具體回答第三個重點——打算做什麼——用了兩個具體的計畫（吃芋圓、拍照），不是空泛的『have fun』。
CommunicativeIt should be a really relaxing day out for the whole class.
多加一句個人感想，讓文章不只是機械式回答四點，更像是真心在跟朋友分享期待。
OrganisationBy the way, where did your class go on your last school trip, and did you enjoy it?
用 By the way 這個轉折語自然切換話題，同時完成第四個重點——反問 Olivia 的問題。
CommunicativePlease write back and tell me everything!
用邀請對方回信的句子收尾，讓這封信讀起來像真正的雙向對話。
OrganisationLove, Amy
有署名，首尾呼應完整。
最常見的失分
  • 只回答『去哪裡』卻沒說明『為什麼選那裡』（第 2 點），Content 不完整。
  • 原因寫得含糊，例如只寫『because it's fun』，沒有具體理由，無法完全說服讀者，Communicative Achievement 受影響。
  • 信末問句寫成直述句（例如寫成 'Olivia's last school trip was fun.' 而不是問句），沒有真正達成『詢問』這個語言功能。
  • 地名等專有名詞大小寫錯誤（如 jiufen 沒有大寫開頭），看起來不夠自然，會被視為 Language 的小失分點。
題目 3

Choosing a class gift

Task 1

This is an email from your English friend, Jack.
Write an email to Jack.
In your email, remember to:
- say what gift your class chose for your teacher
- explain why your class chose that gift
- say how your class collected the money for it
- give Jack an idea for his own class's gift
Write about 100 words.

Hi! Guess what -- I heard your homeroom teacher is moving to a different school, so your class is buying her a leaving gift! What did you choose in the end? My class needs to buy a gift for our teacher too, but nobody can agree on anything! Any ideas would really help. From, Jack
  • say what gift your class chose
  • explain why your class chose that gift
  • say how your class collected the money for it
  • give Jack an idea for his own class's gift

Write about 100 words.

0 字 目標 約 100 字
交卷前先自己檢查
看範文與逐句解析（102 字）
OrganisationHi Jack,
稱呼對應朋友名字，格式正確。
ContentThanks for your email; it's true that Ms. Chen is leaving, so we bought her a beautiful photo album.
先確認對方聽到的消息屬實，再具體回答第一個重點——選了什麼禮物，資訊明確不含糊。
ContentWe chose it because we filled it with photos and messages from every student in the class.
具體說明第二個重點——為什麼選這個禮物——用細節（每個學生的照片和留言）讓理由有說服力。
LanguageTo collect the money, each of us gave fifty dollars, and our class leader kept it safe in an envelope.
句首用不定詞片語 To collect the money 加上並列子句，句型比單純的簡單句複雜，同時也交代了第三個重點的做法。
ContentFor your class, maybe you could try something similar, like a scrapbook or a small potted plant.
直接給 Jack 一個具體、現成可用的點子（剪貼簿或小盆栽），回答第四個重點，用 maybe you could 這種建議句型語氣得體。
CommunicativePlants are cheap, and they remind teachers of their students for a long time.
多加一句理由說服 Jack 這個建議值得參考，讓建議更有說服力，不只是丟一個點子就結束。
CommunicativeI really hope your class can agree on something soon!
用關心對方處境的話收尾，呼應朋友信裡『nobody can agree』的抱怨，顯示有認真讀對方的信。
OrganisationGood luck, Tom
署名收尾，信件結構完整。
最常見的失分
  • 禮物寫得太籠統（只寫『a nice gift』而沒有具體說是什麼），Content 細節不足。
  • 忘記說明『怎麼收錢』（第 3 點），這是很多學生會漏掉的細節重點。
  • 給 Jack 的建議只是重複自己班上的做法，卻沒有用 could/maybe 這類建議句型，語氣不像在給建議，Communicative Achievement 受影響。
  • 金額或數字寫成不自然的格式（如 50$ 而不是 fifty dollars），不符合英文書寫習慣。
題目 4

Studying for an exam (international pen friend)

Task 1

This is an email from your international pen friend, Sofia.
Write an email to Sofia.
In your email, remember to:
- say what method or app you are using to study English
- say if it is helpful and explain why
- give Sofia one study tip
- ask Sofia when her next exam is
Write about 100 words.

Hi! I remember you said you were studying hard for an English exam. How is it going? Have you found a good way to study, like an app or something else? I'm also getting ready for some exams here in Spain, so a study tip from you would be amazing! Lots of love, Sofia
  • say what method or app you are using to study English
  • say if it is helpful and explain why
  • give Sofia one study tip
  • ask Sofia when her next exam is

Write about 100 words.

0 字 目標 約 100 字
交卷前先自己檢查
看範文與逐句解析（105 字）
OrganisationHi Sofia,
稱呼對應朋友名字，格式正確。
CommunicativeIt's great to hear from you, and thanks for asking about my studying!
先感謝對方的關心再回答問題，語氣友善自然，符合寫給朋友的信該有的口吻。
ContentAt the moment, I'm using a vocabulary app every evening and writing new words in a small notebook.
具體回答第一個重點——用什麼方法讀書——講出實際做法（app+筆記本），不是只寫『I study a lot』。
LanguageIt's really helpful because the app tests me on the same words again a few days later, so I don't forget them.
用 because 加上 so 兩層子句解釋原因和結果，句子結構有深度，同時完整回答第二個重點。
ContentMy tip for you is to study a little every day instead of studying for hours all at once.
給 Sofia 一個具體、可執行的讀書技巧（少量多餐 vs. 一次讀很久），回答第三個重點。
LanguageShort, regular practice always works better than one long session before the exam.
用比較句型（works better than）進一步支持前一句的建議，展現語言廣度，不只是丟一句建議就結束。
ContentWhen is your next exam in Spain?
用正確的問句反問 Sofia，完成第四個重點——詢問對方考試時間。
CommunicativeI hope your studying is going well too!
用關心對方的話收尾，讓信件有來有往，而不是單方面回答問題。
OrganisationTake care, Wei-Chen
署名收尾，信件結構完整。
最常見的失分
  • 只寫『I use an app』卻沒有講清楚是什麼類型的 app 或方法，Content 細節薄弱。
  • 說明『是否有幫助』時沒有給理由（第 2 點），變成只有結論沒有解釋。
  • 給的讀書技巧太籠統（如只寫『study hard』），不是具體、可執行的建議。
  • 現在進行式與現在簡單式混用不一致（例如 I'm using... 又寫成 I use it every evening 兩種時態交錯），Language 會被扣分。

練習之後

寫作是唯一需要有人改的一卷

範文能告訴你「好的長什麼樣子」，但沒辦法告訴你「你自己寫的哪裡不對」——這兩件事差很遠。同一個錯誤重複二十次卻沒人指出來，是自學寫作最常見的結局。如果是文法拖累的，可以先對照 PET 文法範圍逐項教學；想要有人真的批改，我們的課每週都在做這件事。
