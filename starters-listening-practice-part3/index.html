官方題型說明 Task format

官方題型：listen and tick the box (3 picture options)。整份聽力卷約 20 分鐘。Starters 適合約 6–8 歲的孩子，每個 Part 的題號都從 1 開始，和官方一樣。

題目說明（rubric）依照官方題型格式以英文呈現；以下練習題為埃森美語原創編寫，用於熟悉題型，非劍橋官方試題。官方免費樣卷：cambridgeenglish.org → Pre A1 Starters preparation。

關於這些錄音　音檔為合成語音，每個角色用不同的聲音、語速依等級放慢，內容與停頓完全依照官方格式，包含官方規定的「播放兩次」。合成語音再自然也不等於真人錄音——正式考試放的是真人錄的。先自己聽兩次再打開逐字稿。

錄音 1第 1 題0:40　含重複播放
看逐字稿（先自己聽兩次再打開）

NarratorOne. What is Anna's favourite toy?

WomanAnna, do you play with your doll?

ManYes. But my kite is my favourite. I fly it in the park.

WomanWhat about your train?

ManThat's my brother's.

NarratorNow listen to Part 3 again.

WomanAnna, do you play with your doll?

ManYes. But my kite is my favourite. I fly it in the park.

WomanWhat about your train?

ManThat's my brother's.

1. What is Anna's favourite toy?

A：✗ 陷阱：娃娃有提到，但 'Yes' 不是最喜歡。

B：✓ 'my kite is my favourite'。

C：✗ 陷阱：火車是哥哥的，不是 Anna 的。

NarratorTwo. Where is the ball?

WomanIs the ball on the chair?

ManNo. Look in the box.

WomanIn the box? Not under the table?

ManNo, it's in the box.

NarratorNow listen to Part 3 again.

WomanIs the ball on the chair?

ManNo. Look in the box.

WomanIn the box? Not under the table?

ManNo, it's in the box.

2. Where is the ball?

A：✗ 陷阱：椅子有提到，但答案是 No。

B：✗ 陷阱：桌子下面也有提到，也是 No。

C：✓ 'Look in the box.' 而且最後又說一次 'it's in the box'。

A：✓ 'Bread today.'

B：✗ 陷阱：蘋果是早上吃的，不是午餐。

C：✗ 陷阱：'No cake?' — 'Not today.'
